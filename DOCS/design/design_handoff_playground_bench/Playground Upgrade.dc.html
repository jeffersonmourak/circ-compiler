No boxes, one surfaceThe four bordered panes become two regions on one sheet with a single hairline between them. Source on --pane-bg, circuit on --bg with a dot grid: the two materials do the separating.
Sidebar → switcherThe 13rem tree is used once per session, then sits there. It becomes a breadcrumb popover in the top bar; the width goes to the circuit. Files are tabs over the source, as they were before the tree.
Data becomes a floating panelThe pin table leaves the tab strip and floats over the canvas, opened from one Data button that carries the pin count. Single-bit inputs still toggle by clicking the pin itself; the panel is for typing bus values and reading many outputs at once. Views are one segmented control: Schematic · Live · Truth.
Dock and drawer → two linesDiagnostics are a footer line under the source, expanding on click. The console is a one-line terminal across the bottom that grows on focus; Memory is a badge in it. Nothing is open until you ask.
